🛠️ Standard Item & Build Suite

1. On-Field Hypercarries

  • Artifact Set: Marechaussee Hunter (HP manipulation), Obsidian Codex (Natlan DPS), or signature 4-piece sets.
  • Main Stats: Sands: ATK% / HP% / EM | Goblet: Elemental DMG Bonus | Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG
  • Sub-stats Priority: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG > Main Stat % > Energy Recharge (ER)
  • Weapons: Signature 5-Star > Sacrificial Jade / Deathmatch (BP) > Flowing Purity / Whiteblind (Craftable)

2. Off-Field Sub-DPS

  • Artifact Set: Golden Troupe (Elemental Skill damage) or Emblem of Severed Fate (Elemental Burst damage).
  • Main Stats: Sands: ER% / HP% / ATK% | Goblet: Elemental DMG Bonus | Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG
  • Sub-stats Priority: Energy Recharge (until target) > CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG > Main Stat %
  • Weapons: Signature 5-Star > Favonius Series > The Stringless / Fleuve Cendre Ferryman

3. Universal Supports & Buffers

  • Artifact Set: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (Natlan supports), Viridescent Venerer (Anemo), or Noblesse Oblige.
  • Main Stats: Sands: ER% / EM | Goblet: EM / HP% | Circlet: EM / Healing Bonus / CRIT Rate (for Favonius)
  • Sub-stats Priority: Energy Recharge > Elemental Mastery / HP%
  • Weapons: Favonius Series, Freedom-Sworn, Skyward Blade

⚡ Energy Recharge (ER) Thresholds

  • Solo Anemo / Geo / Dendro Supports: 180% – 220% ER
  • Off-field Burst Sub-DPS (e.g., Xiangling, Yelan): 200% – 240% ER (reduce by ~20% with Raiden or Favonius weapons)
  • On-Field Carries with low Burst costs: 110% – 130% ER
